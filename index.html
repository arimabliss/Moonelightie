Happy Birthday, Samay ❤️
Dear Samay,

Happy Birthday, bhai. ❤️

Pata nahi yeh letter kahan se shuru karun, kyunki kuch friendships ko words mein properly explain karna genuinely difficult hota hai. Humari friendship ko kitna time hua, woh important nahi hai, but the memories, the laughs, the random conversations, and all the moments we've shared definitely are.

Sabse pehle, thank you. Thank you meri life ka part banne ke liye. Shayad main har baar express nahi karta, but I genuinely appreciate having a friend like you in my life.

Tu sirf ek normal friend nahi hai. Tu un logon mein se hai jinke saath bina kisi reason ke bhi baat kar sakta hoon, stupidest jokes pe has sakta hoon, random topics pe hours tak bakchodi kar sakta hoon, aur kabhi kabhi bina kuch bole bhi comfortable feel kar sakta hoon.

Humne saath mein jo bhi memories banayi hain, unmein se kuch absolutely stupid hain, kuch unforgettable hain, aur kuch aisi hain jo shayad future mein yaad karke hum dono hasenge. But honestly, wahi moments friendship ko special banate hain.

Life mein bohot log aate hain aur chale jaate hain, but kuch log aise hote hain jinke saath ek genuine bond ban jaata hai. Tu mere liye unhi logon mein se ek hai.

Main shayad har baar nahi bolta, but I'm genuinely proud to call you my friend. Aur chahe life kitni bhi change ho jaaye, I hope humari friendship kabhi un random conversations, stupid jokes aur endless bakchodi ko lose na kare.

Aur haan, problems toh life mein aayengi hi. Kabhi sab kuch perfect nahi hoga. But whenever things get difficult, I hope you remember ki tere paas aise log hain jo genuinely tere liye care karte hain. And yes, main bhi unmein se ek hoon.

Thank you for all the laughs.
Thank you for all the memories.
Thank you for all the random conversations.
Thank you for all the stupid moments.
Aur sabse zyada, thank you for being the person you are.

I hope this birthday becomes another memory that we'll look back at years later and smile about.

Happy Birthday once again, Samay! ❤️

May this year bring you everything you've been working for, lots of happiness, countless reasons to smile, and people who always remind you how valuable you are.

Stay the same bakchod you've always been. 😂❤️

Your papa ji,
Always. ❤️
