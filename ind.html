Happy 3 Months, Archita ❤️
Dear Archita,

Happy 3 Months, meri jaan. ❤️

Pata nahi yeh letter kahan se shuru karun, kyunki sirf 3 mahine hue hain, lekin aisa lagta hai jaise aap meri life ka hamesha se hissa rahi ho.

Sabse pehle, thank you. Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye. In 3 mahino mein aapne meri life ko jitna beautiful banaya hai, shayad main kabhi words mein properly explain hi nahi kar paunga. Aapse milna meri life ka ek aisa chapter hai jise main baar baar padhna chahta hoon.

Aap sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho. Aap meri peace ho, meri comfort ho, meri favourite notification ho, meri smile ka reason ho, aur woh insaan ho jinke saath ek normal sa din bhi special ban jaata hai.

Sirf 3 months hue hain, lekin in 3 months mein maine itni memories collect kar li hain ki kabhi kabhi bas unhe yaad karke hi smile aa jaati hai. Hamari calls, hamari random baatein, hamari hasi, hamare little arguments, hamara pyaar, sab kuch mere liye priceless hai.

Main har din thankful feel karta hoon ki meri life mein aap ho. Shayad mujhe har baar perfectly express karna nahi aata, lekin jitna pyaar main feel karta hoon, woh har din aur badhta hi jaa raha hai.

Mujhe aapki smile bahut pasand hai. Jab aap genuinely hansti ho na, toh lagta hai meri poori duniya thodi aur bright ho gayi. Aur jab aap upset hoti ho, toh bas ek hi wish hoti hai ki kaash main aapka mood instantly theek kar paun aur aapke face par wohi smile wapas laa sakun.

Main perfect nahi hoon. Main galtiyan karta hoon, kabhi kabhi overthink karta hoon, kabhi aapko bina intention ke hurt bhi kar deta hoon. Lekin ek promise hamesha rahega, main har din better partner banne ki koshish karunga. Aapke liye. Humare liye.

In 3 months maine ek cheez zaroor seekhi hai, pyaar sirf "I love you" bolne se nahi hota. Pyaar hota hai care karne se, samajhne se, wait karne se, respect dene se, aur har situation mein ek dusre ka saath dene se. Aur main chahta hoon ki hum hamesha isi tarah saath grow karein.

Mujhe nahi pata future mein life humein kahan le jaayegi. Shayad easy days bhi aayenge aur difficult days bhi. Lekin meri ek hi wish hai, chahe jo bhi ho, hum dono ek dusre ka haath kabhi na chhodein.

Agar kabhi aapko lage ki duniya aapke against hai, ya sab kuch bahut difficult ho gaya hai, toh bas ek baat yaad rakhna, aap akeli kabhi nahi ho. Main hamesha aapke saath hoon. Har smile mein. Har problem mein. Har achievement mein. Har struggle mein.

Aaj sirf hamare 3 months complete hue hain, lekin meri dua hai ki yeh 3 months ek din 3 years banen, phir 30 years, aur phir ek poori lifetime.

Thank you for every smile you've given me.
Thank you for every memory we've made.
Thank you for choosing me.
Aur sabse zyada, thank you for being you.

Happy 3 Months once again, meri Archita. ❤️

May we continue making countless memories together, keep laughing at the silliest things, support each other through every phase of life, and never stop choosing each other.

I love you more than yesterday, and I'll love you even more tomorrow.

Forever yours,
